# Code of Conduct

> **Portuguese version:** [Código de Conduta](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, appearance, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for different experiences and opinions.
- Accept technical feedback openly and provide constructive criticism.
- Prioritize what is best for the community and user safety.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and correct negative impact.

Harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized language, disclosure of private information, and any conduct that creates a hostile environment are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Inappropriate behavior may be reported privately to maintainer `@luizrn`. The maintainer will investigate confidentially and may edit or remove content, issue warnings, or permanently restrict participation according to severity.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the community.
